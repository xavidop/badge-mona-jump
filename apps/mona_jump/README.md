# Mona Jump

Bounce ever higher from platform to platform, tilting the badge to steer, and see how high an Octocat can climb.

Green platforms are solid, blue ones slide, brown ones crumble after one
bounce, and springs launch Mona sky high. Fall off the bottom and the climb is
over; the badge keeps your best height.

## Controls

* Tilt, or Left / Right: steer
* Select: start; jump again after a fall
* Menu: switch between tilt and button steering (title screen)
* Back: back to the title

## Requirements

No network. Best height and climb count are saved under `/state`. The sprites
in `assets/` are drawn by the repository's `tools/build_sprites.py`.
